r8bit Support

Last updated: 12 August 2026

r8bit is a mobile app for cataloguing the video games you own, rating them, and tracking how far through them you are.

Need help? Email dan@danhug.com and you'll get a reply as soon as possible. Including your device model and iOS version helps.

Common questions

A game is missing from the search results.
Search results come from the IGDB games database, which doesn't include every title. You can still add the game manually — enter the name yourself and fill in whichever details you want.
A game has the wrong cover art or developer.
Open the game, choose Edit, and correct any field by hand. You can also re-run the IGDB match to pick a different entry.
My games didn't appear after signing in on another device.
Your library is tied to your account, so make sure you're signed in with the same email address. If it's still empty, get in touch.
How do I delete my account?
In the app, go to Account → Delete account. This permanently removes your login and every game in your library straight away, and cannot be undone. If you'd rather we did it for you, email dan@danhug.com.
How do I stop seeing personalised ads?
On iOS, go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking and turn off permission for r8bit. You'll still see ads, but they won't be personalised. In the EEA, UK, and Switzerland you can also change your consent choices from within the app.
Can I use r8bit without an account?
Not currently — your library is stored against your account so it's available whenever you sign in, on any device.
