RateBit Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 August 2026

RateBit is a mobile app for cataloguing the video games you own and tracking how you play them. This policy explains what the app collects, why, and who it is shared with.

Who we are

RateBit is operated by Daniel Hughes. For any privacy question or request, contact dan@danhug.com.

What we collect

DataWhy
Email address and password To create and secure your account. Passwords are hashed by our authentication provider and are never visible to us.
Your game library — game titles, developer/publisher, platform, play status, rating, playtime, start and finish dates, purchase date, cost, and any notes you write This is the content of the app. It is stored against your account so it is available when you sign in.
Game searches When you search for a game to add, the search text is sent to the IGDB game database to return matching titles and cover art.
Advertising identifiers, approximate location derived from IP address, device information, and ad interactions To display advertising. Collected by Google AdMob, not by us directly. See “Advertising” below.
Your sign-in session and app preferences Stored only on your device so you stay signed in and so advertising frequency limits are respected.

We do not collect your name, precise location, contacts, photos, or health data. We do not sell your personal information.

Advertising

RateBit displays advertising through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use device identifiers — including the Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) on iOS — to measure and personalise ads.

On iOS you will be asked for permission before any tracking identifier is used, through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you decline, you will still see ads, but they will not be personalised. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, you will be shown a consent form before advertising is loaded, and you can change your choices from within the app. Where you do not consent, personalised advertising is not requested.

Google's handling of this data is governed by the Google Privacy & Terms.

Who your data is shared with

ServicePurpose
Supabase Hosts your account and game library.
Google AdMob Serves advertising in the app.
IGDB (Twitch) Provides game titles, developers, platforms, and cover art when you search.

Your game library is private to your account and is not shared with other users.

How long we keep it

Your account and library are kept until you delete them. Deleting a game removes it immediately.

You can delete your entire account from inside the app, under Account → Delete account. This permanently removes your login and every game in your library straight away, and cannot be undone. If you would rather we did it for you, email dan@danhug.com.

Your rights

You can view and edit your library at any time inside the app. Depending on where you live — including under the UK GDPR, EU GDPR, and the California Consumer Privacy Act — you may also have the right to request a copy of your data, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, or object to how it is used. Email dan@danhug.com to make a request.

Children

RateBit is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and it will be deleted.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will be updated. Significant changes will be communicated in the app.
